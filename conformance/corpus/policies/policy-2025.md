# Refund Policy 2025

Applies to purchases made from 1 January 2025.

## Eligibility

Items must be unused and in original packaging. Proof of purchase is required.

## Refund window

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of delivery. From day 15 to day 30 a store credit is issued instead. After 30 days no refund or credit is available.

## Store credits

Store credits expire 12 months after issue and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Contact

Email refunds@example.com with your order number.
