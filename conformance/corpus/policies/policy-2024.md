# Refund Policy 2024

Applies to purchases made from 1 January 2024.

## Eligibility

Items must be unused and in original packaging. Proof of purchase is required.

## Refund window

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of delivery. After 30 days no refund is available.

## Shipping costs

Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged.

## Contact

Email refunds@example.com with your order number.
