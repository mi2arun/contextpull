# Frequently asked questions

## Can I run an R-40 outdoors?

Only inside an enclosure rated IP54 or better. The operating range is -5 to 40 °C; see the specification sheet for the full table.

## What does WR-2201 mean?

It is the controller's surge event code. Operation above the rated voltage voids the warranty; see the current warranty policy for the threshold.

## Which units are ATEX rated?

The R-110 and R-120 carry ATEX Zone 2. No other model is rated for explosive atmospheres.

## Is the sensor harness user-serviceable?

Yes, procedure P-40 covers it. The brake pads on the drive unit (PN-88120) are also user-replaceable.
