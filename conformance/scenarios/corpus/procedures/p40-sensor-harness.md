# P-40: Reseating the sensor harness

Applies when WR-2205 is shown. Estimated time 20 minutes. Requires torque driver and ESD strap.

## Safety

Disconnect mains and wait 5 minutes for the capacitors to discharge before opening the cabinet. Confirm 0 V at test point TP7.

## Step 1: open the cabinet

Remove the four M5 cover screws (torque 3 Nm on refit). Store the screws in the magnetic tray.

## Step 2: locate the harness

The sensor harness is the 12-way grey connector on the left of the controller board, labelled J4.

## Step 3: reseat

Release the latch, pull the connector straight out, inspect pins for corrosion, and push back until the latch clicks. Do not twist.

## Step 4: verify

Restore mains. The controller must boot without WR-2205 within 60 seconds. If WR-2205 persists, escalate under procedure P-41.

## Step 5: close

Refit the cover; torque the M5 screws to 3 Nm. Record the service in the log with the unit serial.
