# Warranty Policy 2025

Applies to R-series units shipped from 1 January 2025.

## Coverage period

The standard warranty covers manufacturing defects for **36 months** from the delivery date. Consumables (filters, gaskets, batteries) are covered for 90 days.

## Claim window

A claim must be opened within 14 days of the fault being observed. From day 15 to day 45 a claim may still be opened but is limited to repair, not replacement. Claims after day 45 are not accepted.

## Exclusions

Damage from liquid ingress, operation above 250 V, or unauthorised repair is excluded. Diagnostic codes WR-2201 (surge event) and WR-2210 (thermal cut-out override) void the claim automatically.

## Replacement parts

Replacement parts carry the remainder of the original warranty or 12 months, whichever is longer.
