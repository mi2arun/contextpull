# Warranty Policy 2024

Applies to R-series units shipped between 1 January 2024 and 31 December 2024.

## Coverage period

The standard warranty covers manufacturing defects for **24 months** from the delivery date. Consumables (filters, gaskets, batteries) are covered for 90 days.

## Claim window

A claim must be opened within 30 days of the fault being observed. Claims opened later are assessed at the service centre's discretion.

## Exclusions

Damage from liquid ingress, operation above 240 V, or unauthorised repair is excluded. Diagnostic code WR-2201 (surge event) voids the claim automatically.

## Replacement parts

Replacement parts carry the remainder of the original warranty or 6 months, whichever is longer.
